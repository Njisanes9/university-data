# Higher Education & Training Dashboard (Power BI)
# Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of South Africa’s Higher Education and Training sector across multiple years. It delivers insights into university performance, student enrollment trends, pass and failure rates, graduation metrics, and demographic distribution.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor institutional performance, compare provinces, and analyze student outcomes over time.

# Key Metrics

The dashboard highlights the following national totals:

- Total Universities: 26

- Total Enrolled Students: 7.5 million

- Total Graduates: 1.4 million

- Total Passed: 5.6 million

- Total Failed: 1.9 million

Interactive slicers allow filtering by:

- University

- Year

# Dashboard Pages & Insights
# 1. Summary Page

The Summary page provides a high-level overview of:

- Total universities and student counts

- Enrollment, pass, and failure trends over time

- Provincial success rates

- Student racial distribution trends

Key Insights:

- Enrollment steadily increased from 2009 to around 2013–2015 before slightly stabilizing.

- Total passes significantly exceed total failures across all years.

- Provincial success rates range roughly between 63% and 70%, with Gauteng and Western Cape among the highest-performing provinces.

- African students represent the largest demographic group across all years.

# 2. Enrollment Analysis

This page focuses on student enrollment patterns:

# Enrollment by Year

- Enrollment increased from approximately 838K (2009) to a peak near 985K (2015).

- A slight dip is visible toward 2016.

# Enrollment by Province

- Gauteng leads in total enrollments.

- Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal follow closely.

- Northern Cape shows the lowest enrollment figures.

# Enrollment by University

Universities such as:

- University of South Africa (UNISA)

- University of Cape Town

- University of Stellenbosch

- Cape Peninsula University of Technology
show high enrollment volumes.

# Enrollment by Race

- African students form the majority.

- Coloured, White, and Indian student groups show stable but smaller proportions.

- “Other” represents a minimal share.

# 3. Performance Analysis

This section evaluates academic outcomes:

# Total Passed vs ❌ Total Failed by Year

- Pass numbers increased steadily from 2009 to 2015.

- Failures remain significantly lower than passes but show moderate fluctuation.

- The overall national success rate averages around 74%.

# Performance by University

- Success rates vary by institution.

- Several universities maintain pass rates above 80%.

- North-West University and others show comparatively lower success rates.

# Performance by Province

- Gauteng records the highest total passes.

- Provincial comparisons reveal both volume and performance disparities.

# Graduates

- Proportion of Graduates by Province – Pie chart showing percentage distribution across provinces.

- Total Graduates by Province – Interactive map visualizing geographic distribution.

- Total Graduates by Year – Trend analysis highlighting graduate growth over time.

- University & Year Breakdown Table – Detailed view of graduate counts per university and year.
